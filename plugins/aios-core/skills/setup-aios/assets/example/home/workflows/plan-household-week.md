# Plan the household week

Produce an agreed household plan for the coming Monday through Sunday.
The [weekly routine](../routines/weekly-household-review.md) sets the meeting time and participants.

## Procedure

1. Read the previous weekly plan and ask Alex and Sam what actually happened.
2. Check the [family schedule](../context/schedule.md) against changes they report for the coming week.
3. Assign an adult to every pickup and activity, including travel time and any handoff.
4. Resolve conflicts with the [shared planning commitments](../../shared/docs/planning.md) before adding optional household work.
5. Choose five weekday dinners using the [meal constraints](../context/meals.md) and current pantry inventory.
6. Make the grocery list and mark product or price questions for adult review before purchase.
7. Choose the next household project action and time for [personal goals](../goals/walking-and-reading.md).
8. Confirm assignments with Alex and Sam, leaving unresolved arrangements as named open actions.
9. Write the dated weekly plan in `docs/`, with owners, dates, meals, and preparation needs.
10. Record the review in `journal/`, separating observations, decisions, and open actions.
11. Give Alex the agreed time commitments for [shared weekly planning](../../shared/workflows/weekly-review.md).

## Completion check

- Every school pickup and activity has a named adult with feasible travel time.
- The plan fits work and studio commitments without assuming another adult is available.
- Ingredient and product questions remain visible until Alex or Sam resolves them.
- Open actions have an owner, a due date, and evidence that will show completion.
- Recorded goal progress reflects completed sessions rather than scheduled ones.
