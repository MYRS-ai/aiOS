# Home glossary

Terms Alex and Sam use for household planning.

| Term | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Entryway reset** | The project to give school bags, shoes, and activity equipment fixed storage places by the front door. | Ends after five school mornings meet the project's completion criteria. |
| **Household review** | Alex and Sam's Sunday meeting to agree on the following week's family responsibilities. | The routine schedules it; the planning workflow gives its steps. |
| **Weekly plan** | The dated document recording one week's household commitments, meals, and assigned open actions. | Use the week beginning Monday in its filename. |
