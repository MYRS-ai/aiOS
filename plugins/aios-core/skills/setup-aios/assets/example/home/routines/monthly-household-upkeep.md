Alex checks a small set of household items and records any work that needs attention.

| Setting | Arrangement |

## Scope

- Look for visible drips below accessible kitchen and bathroom sinks.
- Check the entryway for a clear walking route and a freely opening door.
- Review household supplies and list needed replacements before purchase.
- Review unresolved household actions and choose their owners and dates.

Record observations without treating a visual check as professional inspection.
Work requiring specialist knowledge becomes a separate action for Alex to assess.

The October check used 11:30–12:00. The entryway project continued afterward until 12:30.
No recurring reminder or service booking is connected.
