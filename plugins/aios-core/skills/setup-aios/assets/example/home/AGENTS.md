Alex Rivera's family life, household upkeep, and personal goals. Alex and Sam share care for Maya and Leo; Elena lives nearby.

## Instructions

### Plan around the family

- Read the family schedule before proposing a commitment. School pickups and activities need a named adult.
- Keep Tuesday and Thursday evenings free after 19:00 for Alex's studio work.
- Follow the meal constraints when preparing meal plans or grocery lists. Alex or Sam checks unfamiliar products before purchase.

### Keep plans grounded

- Treat the dated weekly plan as the current household plan. Confirm changes with Alex before changing assignments.
- Record an unresolved arrangement as an open action with an owner and a date.
- Use the monthly upkeep routine for repeated checks. Keep household work with an end in projects.
