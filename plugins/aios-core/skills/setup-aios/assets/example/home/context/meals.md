# Meals and groceries

Plan five weekday dinners, use existing groceries first, and leave room for weekend choices.

## Household constraints

- Maya has a peanut allergy. Do not include peanuts or peanut ingredients in household meal suggestions.
- Alex or Sam checks ingredients and the household's established allergy requirements before using an unfamiliar product.
- A recipe name or prior purchase does not confirm a product's ingredients. Mark uncertain items for adult review.
- Keep meal planning within these recorded preferences; do not infer medical guidance from them.

## Preferences and workload

| Topic | Preference |
|---|---|
| Weekday preparation | About 30 minutes, using familiar recipes |
| Vegetables | Offer a familiar vegetable beside a new one |
| Tuesday and Thursday | Sam cooks while Alex handles soccer |
| Leftovers | Plan their use explicitly rather than adding another full dinner |
| Shopping | One weekly list; Alex checks the pantry before buying |
| Spending | Aim for a weekly grocery list under $140; confirm the actual total before purchase |

Regular choices include bean tacos, baked potatoes, tomato pasta, chicken with rice, and vegetable soup.
These dish names describe preferences, not approved products or allergy assurances.

## Planning procedure

Meal planning is part of [planning the household week](../workflows/plan-household-week.md).
Record the week's menu, inventory, grocery list, and unresolved items in the dated weekly plan.

The [October 12 plan](../docs/2026-10-12-week-plan.md) uses rice, pasta, canned beans, and frozen peas already on hand.
