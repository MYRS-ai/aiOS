# Family

Alex and Sam share a household with Maya, age 10, and Leo, age 7. Alex's parent Elena lives nearby.

## Responsibilities

| Person | Regular responsibility | Planning detail |
|---|---|---|
| Alex | Weekly plan, Monday/Wednesday/Friday dinners, and household projects | Works weekdays 09:00–17:00; studio time is separately committed |
| Sam | School pickups Monday–Thursday and Tuesday/Thursday dinners | Owns changes to these pickup arrangements |
| Maya | Pack the school bag and put sports equipment away | Chooses one family dinner each week |
| Leo | Put shoes away and help set the table | Needs an adult to check the school bag |
| Elena | Friday school pickup | Additional help requires a separate agreement |

Alex and Sam review the division of work each Sunday. A usual assignment does not imply availability for extra work.

## Preferences

- Keep weekday dinners simple, with preparation taking about 30 minutes.
- Keep Saturday afternoon available for family time instead of assigning every hour.
- Plan one shared outing each month. Decide its cost and travel before committing.
- Keep a quiet reading period after the children go to bed.
- Choose gifts together; purchases need Alex's approval.
