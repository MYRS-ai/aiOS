# Job note observations, 2026-10-07

Alex and Nora reviewed how four technicians and two dispatch supervisors understood note delivery. The session used a text-note walkthrough, not a live customer rollout.

## Scope and source

Participants are recorded as technicians T1 through T4 and supervisors D1 and D2. This entry summarizes Alex's and Nora's notes from the session.

The underlying recording and verbatim transcript are not available here. Statements below are paraphrased observations, not direct quotations.

## Observations

| Observation | Participants | Implication to test |
|---|---|---|
| A saved indicator was interpreted as confirmation that the dispatcher could read the note. | T1 and T3 | Separate local save from completed send. |
| The technician checked for a connection before trusting the note's delivery state. | T2 and T4 | Keep unsent text visible and explain what the app will do next. |
| Repeated notes made it unclear whether a technician had new information. | D1 and D2 | Show a single note when a submission is retried. |

These observations concern the six participants in this session. They do not measure the frequency of the problem across customers.

## Decisions and follow-up

- Alex kept the first project scope to text notes attached to an existing visit.
- Nora will refine the saved, waiting, and sent labels and plan a follow-up observation.
- Sam will include an interrupted-response scenario in the preliminary test run.
