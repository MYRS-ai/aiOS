# Mobile job notes

The project to let technicians record text notes during a visit and send them reliably when connectivity returns.

## Instructions

### Use the current record

Read [brief.md](brief.md) for the outcome and current state. Treat [evidence.md](evidence.md) as the record of observations, not a promise of readiness.

### Keep pilot decisions explicit

Follow the [readiness review](../../workflows/readiness-review.md) before recommending a pilot start. Update the brief and plan after a decision is recorded in the journal.
