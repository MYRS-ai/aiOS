# Review studio commitments

Turn the week's evidence into a feasible plan for the next week. The [routine](../routines/studio-review.md) sets the schedule; this workflow describes the steps.

1. Confirm next week's available blocks with Alex's supplied calendar changes. Start from the [capacity plan](../context/capacity.md).
2. Compare each client commitment with its project state. Identify a decision or delivery at risk before selecting product work.
3. Review new product evidence. Separate completed tests from intentions, informal interest, and unapproved spending.
4. Choose work that fits six hours or the reduced available time. Reserve the next review's half-hour inside that total.
5. Record the review in `journal/` with its date, evidence, decisions, and next actions. Give each action an owner and a date.
6. Update current project, product, and capacity docs to match the decisions. Keep the journal entry as the dated record.
7. Carry commitments and unresolved calendar conflicts into the [shared planning procedure](../../shared/workflows/weekly-review.md).

## Completion check

- The time allocation includes review time and does not assume extra evenings.
- Every external action remains a draft unless Alex approved that action.
- Deferred work is explicit, so its absence from the next week is intentional.
- Each decision points to available evidence or states what remains unknown.

The [October 9 review](../journal/2026-10-09-studio-review.md) records a completed use of this workflow.
