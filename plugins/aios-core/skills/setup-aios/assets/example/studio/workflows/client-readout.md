# Prepare a client decision readout

Help a client choose a next action from the available evidence. A polished recommendation must still expose uncertainty and alternatives.

1. Read the client's contract and project. Confirm the question, permitted material, scope, and decision owner.
2. Trace each material claim to a research note. Remove claims that depend on unavailable sources or mark them as unverified.
3. Compare at least two credible options, including stopping or retaining the current process when the evidence supports it.
4. Draft one recommendation with its limits, the decision requested, and the proposed next action.
5. Ask Alex to approve the version and recipients before sending or sharing it. Keep the draft local until approval arrives.
6. Record the client's decision after Alex supplies the discussion outcome. Do not infer acceptance from silence or a scheduled meeting.
7. Update the project with the decision, owner, date, and any remaining obligation. Treat additional work as a separate scope decision.
