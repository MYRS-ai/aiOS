## Proposed trial

Use one shared record per consenting studio for two weeks. Record the owner's current process before the trial begins.

Mina would own the trial and participant communication. Each owner would retain control of customer information. Alex's current engagement ends with the decision readout.

Assess these questions at the end:

- Did the owner reconcile credits with less effort than during the recorded starting period?
- Did another staff member understand the balances without Mina explaining them?
- Which exceptions still required a separate conversation?

Treat reported time savings as directional unless owners record comparable periods. A successful trial would justify another decision, not automatically justify software development.
