# Willow Labs discovery sprint

Help Mina Shah choose a next experiment for make-up credits by October 16, 2026. The decision is whether to test a manual service, investigate another problem, or stop.

**Status on October 9:** Research synthesis is complete. The readout is drafted and has not been sent. The founder discussion is proposed for October 13.

## Starting question

Willow Labs initially proposed a booking platform for small ceramics studios. Mina believes expired credits and rescheduled classes cause enough work to justify a dedicated product.

The sprint narrows that belief to one question. Can owners reduce reconciliation work by managing make-up credits in one visible record?

## Scope

- Review four interview summaries supplied by Mina.
- Interview two additional owners about recent credit reconciliation.
- Compare the evidence with simpler alternatives, including the tools owners already use.
- Produce a decision readout with one recommended experiment and explicit limits.

The engagement excludes product design, engineering, software procurement, and participant recruitment for a later experiment. A new experiment requires a separate scope decision.

## Evidence so far

The [research notes](research-notes.md) contain six owner summaries. Four owners described reconciliation or credit-policy problems; two did not.

The strongest examples concern reconciling credits before a new class series. They do not establish demand for a replacement booking platform.

Two owners expressed interest in a manual trial. Interest is not consent to participate, a purchase commitment, or evidence of willingness to pay.
