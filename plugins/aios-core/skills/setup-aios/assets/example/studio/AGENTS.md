# Rivera Studio workspace

Alex Rivera's independent business combines product discovery advisory, digital products, and Tally, a small e-ink desk display. Studio work fits around Alex's job and family commitments.

## Instructions

### Respect client boundaries

Keep client evidence in that client's folder. Get Alex's approval before sending client messages, sharing research, or turning client material into product examples.

### Keep commitments explicit

Draft proposals, purchases, and publication plans for Alex to review. Do not promise delivery dates, accept work, publish products, or place orders without Alex's approval.

### Use the available time

Read [capacity](context/capacity.md) before proposing commitments. Finish the active client decision before adding another client or launching a product.
