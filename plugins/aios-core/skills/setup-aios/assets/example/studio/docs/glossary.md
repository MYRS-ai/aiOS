# Rivera Studio glossary

Terms used in the studio's [advisory work](#advisory-work) and [products](#products). The [shared glossary](../../shared/docs/glossary.md) defines terms used across Alex's domains.

## Advisory work

| Term | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision readout** | A client-facing explanation of evidence, options, and a recommended next action. | Its preparation does not mean the client accepted the recommendation. |
| **Discovery brief** | A statement of the product decision being investigated, the assumptions behind it, and the investigation's limits. | Discovery Kit includes a template for writing one. |
| **Discovery sprint** | A bounded advisory engagement that ends with a product decision readout. | The Willow Labs sprint does not include running the recommended experiment. |
| **Make-up credit** | A ceramics studio customer's right to attend a replacement session after missing a class. | Willow Labs is investigating how studios reconcile these credits. Policies differ between studios. |
| **Manual trial** | A small experiment that delivers a proposed service through people and simple tools before building dedicated software. | Interest in a trial is separate from participant consent. |

## Products

| Term | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Batch** | A planned group of Tally units assembled together. | Batch one proposes 50 units, including replacements and retained units. |
| **Discovery Kit** | Rivera Studio's editable templates for investigating a product decision. | Its examples are independent of client work. |
| **Discovery Kit course** | The planned short course showing how to use Discovery Kit. | The outline exists; recording is paused. |
| **Evidence worksheet** | The kit template that separates observations, interpretations, and remaining questions. | The source and date belong with each observation. |
| **Tally** | Rivera Studio's small e-ink desk display for one manually entered number and label. | The current prototypes update over USB. |
