# Rivera Studio

Rivera Studio helps early founders decide what to test before committing to product development. Alex runs it alongside a full-time product role at Fieldline.

The business has three lines. Advisory work pays for focused research. Digital products make Alex's general discovery methods reusable. Tally explores a physical product with deliberately small production runs.

## Advisory boundaries

Alex advises on product decisions and experiments. A discovery sprint does not include production software, ongoing operations, or an assurance of product demand.

Keep one active client engagement at a time. An additional request stays uncommitted until the current readout is accepted and the capacity review finds room.

Fieldline time, internal documents, equipment, and customer information stay outside studio work. Use only independently developed methods and material Alex can use for the studio.

## Product boundaries

Digital product examples use invented businesses or separately approved public material. Client permission to conduct research does not grant permission to teach from it.

Tally has no public delivery promise, paid preorder, or production order. The [batch plan](../products/tally-batch-one.md) records estimates separately from purchasing decisions.
