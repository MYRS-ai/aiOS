# Studio products

Rivera Studio's digital products and Tally. These docs connect product purpose, current evidence, and the next development decision.

## Instructions

### Separate plans from commitments

Label cost estimates, proposed dates, and untested claims. Alex approves publication, pricing, customer promises, and purchases before they take effect.

### Keep product examples independent

Do not use client names, research, or deliverables in product material without explicit permission. Use the kit's invented examples for development.
