# Discovery Kit course

A short companion course would show a founder using Discovery Kit from an initial question to a decision readout.

**Status on October 9:** Outline only. Recording is paused while the kit's evidence worksheet changes. There are no recorded lessons, enrolled learners, or promised launch dates.

## Intended learner

A founder has an idea and access to potential customers but needs a disciplined first investigation. The course assumes no research training.

The course teaches a small investigation. It does not promise product demand, revenue, or a substitute for specialized research.

## Outline

| Lesson | Learner's result | Planned length |
|---|---|---|
| Frame a decision | A discovery brief with one decision and its limits | 8 minutes |
| Ask about behavior | An interview guide grounded in recent events | 10 minutes |
| Interpret the evidence | A worksheet separating observations and conclusions | 12 minutes |
| Choose the next experiment | A decision readout comparing realistic options | 10 minutes |

The planned runtime is 40 minutes. Exercises use the kit's invented print workshop, so learners can compare their reasoning with a complete example.
