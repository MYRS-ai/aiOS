# Tally batch one

Plan a possible 50-unit Tally batch after prototype testing. Alex has not approved production, ordered batch parts, or accepted preorders.

## Proposed allocation

| Use | Units | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Potential customer units | 45 | Leaves room for support and retained examples |
| Replacement units | 3 | Covers a small initial support reserve |
| Retained units | 2 | Supports troubleshooting and later comparison |
| Total | 50 | The full proposed batch |

The allocation is a planning assumption. Demand and defect rates could require a different quantity or reserve.

## Cost model

Alex's working estimate is $41 per assembled unit, or $2,050 for 50 units. This is an estimate from the prototype model, not a supplier quote.

It excludes postage, selling fees, taxes, Alex's assembly time, and unexpected rework. The two existing prototypes are outside this proposed batch.

No retail price is agreed. Do not describe the difference between a possible price and $41 as profit.

## Conditions before a production decision

1. Record the enclosure test result and resolve any failures.
2. Run repeated display updates and document the observed failure rate.
3. Obtain current part and enclosure quotes with Alex's approval to contact suppliers.
4. Estimate assembly time, packaging, support, and delivery costs.
5. Ask Alex to choose whether to proceed, reduce the batch, or stop.

A quote request is separate from an order. Only Alex can approve the spending amount and place or authorize an order.
